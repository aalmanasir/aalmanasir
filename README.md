## About

I am an Emirati author and independent arbitration researcher based in Abu Dhabi.

My doctoral thesis in International Commercial Arbitration has been accepted, and I am
awaiting the university’s formal documentation. I intend to begin a Fellowship in
International Arbitration at the end of 2026.

قُبلت أطروحتي للدكتوراه في التحكيم التجاري الدولي، وأنتظر الوثائق الرسمية من الجامعة.
وأعتزم بدء زمالة في التحكيم الدولي في نهاية عام 2026.

I founded **The Red Thread**, a bilingual Arabic–English literary project about
memory, identity, belonging, love, time, and the connections that endure between
people. Its six bilingual literary works are published digitally through the official website.

My research focuses on international commercial arbitration, contractual
ambiguity, arbitrator authority, party autonomy, and the legitimacy of legal
decision-making. My professional background is in healthcare support operations
across SEHA and Sheikh Shakhbout Medical City (SSMC).
